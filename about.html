Our Story

It Started With a Dream in 2016

BETH BITES opened its doors in 2016 with a simple promise: cook honest food, season it well, and serve it with a smile. What began as a small kitchen dream has grown into one of the warmest food spots in Wuse II, Abuja, FCT, Nigeria.

From the very first day, our focus has been on real flavour and real hospitality. We cook Nigerian dishes the traditional way, celebrate African cuisine in all its richness, and plate up Intercontinental favourites for customers who want something different. Add fresh pastries, chilled drinks and creamy ice cream — and there is something for everyone.

Today, we still cook every meal like it is for family. Whether you walk in, take away, or ask us to bring it to your door, you get the same care in every single pack.

Meet The Founder

Elizabeth E. — CEO & Senior Chef

The heart of BETH BITES. She leads the team, runs the kitchen, and still tastes every recipe before it reaches your plate.

Founder & Head Chef

"Cooking and having my own restaurant has always been my dream."

For Elizabeth E., food has never just been about eating. It is about the smell of a pot of soup simmering on a Sunday afternoon, the sound of family laughing around a table, and the joy of watching someone take that first satisfying bite.

In 2016, she turned that lifelong dream into BETH BITES. As both CEO and Senior Chef, she is involved in everything — sourcing fresh ingredients, training the kitchen team, perfecting recipes, and making sure every order leaving the restaurant in Wuse II, Abuja meets her standard.

Her vision is simple: to keep serving delicious food that people trust, remember, and come back for — for many more years to come.

"Every plate that leaves my kitchen carries my name. So I cook it the way I would cook for my own family — with love, and with no shortcuts."
Elizabeth E.
CEO & Senior Chef, BETH BITES

What Drives Us

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Three simple things keep BETH BITES moving forward every single day.

Our Mission

To serve fresh, delicious and affordable Nigerian, African and Intercontinental meals — along with pastries, drinks and ice cream — with warm service and dependable home delivery across Abuja.

Our Vision

To become the most loved food brand in Nigeria — a restaurant where families, friends and visitors know they will always find great taste, clean service and a welcoming space.

Our Values

Freshness first. Honest portions. Respect for every customer. Clean kitchens. Consistent taste. And the kind of hospitality that makes you feel at home from the moment you walk in.

Our Journey

From 2016 Till Today

A look at how BETH BITES has grown, one plate at a time.

2016

The Dream Begins

Elizabeth E. opens BETH BITES in Wuse II, Abuja, with a small kitchen, a big recipe book and a clear dream of running her own restaurant.

2017 – 2018

Word Spreads Fast

Customers start coming back weekly for jollof rice, egusi soup and our freshly baked pastries. Word of mouth makes BETH BITES a local favourite.

2019 – 2020

Home Delivery Launched

We add home delivery so families can enjoy BETH BITES meals in their own homes — hot, fresh and carefully packaged.

2021 – 2022

An Expanded Menu

Our kitchen grows to include more African and Intercontinental dishes, alongside drinks, smoothies and creamy ice cream.

2023 – 2024

Events & Catering

Birthdays, weddings, office parties and small chops orders become a big part of what we do — feeding crowds without losing our home-cooked taste.

Today

Still Cooking With Love

Chef Elizabeth and the BETH BITES team continue to serve Abuja daily from Wuse II — with the same dream, the same passion and even better flavours.
