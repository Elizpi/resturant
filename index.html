Our Story

Cooking Has Always Been Her Dream

BETH BITES started in 2016 with one simple belief — that good food should be made with love, served with warmth, and shared with family. What began as a passion in the kitchen has grown into a restaurant that serves hundreds of happy customers every week.

Our CEO, Elizabeth E., is also our Senior Chef. She still personally oversees the kitchen, tasting and perfecting every recipe. Opening her own restaurant has always been her dream — and today she lives it, one delicious plate at a time.
