Quick Answers

A few things people ask us most. If your question is not here, just send us a message — we are happy to help.

WhatsApp is the fastest. Message 0903 296 0035 with what you would like to order, your address and your preferred delivery time. We will confirm your order and total before we start cooking.

We are in Wuse II, Abuja, FCT, Nigeria. Use the map on this page to get directions, or message us on WhatsApp and we will guide you straight to our door.

We deliver to most parts of Abuja including Wuse, Maitama, Garki, Jabi, Utako, Gwarinpa, Life Camp and surrounding areas. Send us your address on WhatsApp and we will confirm availability and the delivery fee immediately.

For large events we recommend booking at least 3 – 5 days ahead. For weddings and very large gatherings, one to two weeks gives us time to plan properly with you. Smaller orders can often be handled same-day.
